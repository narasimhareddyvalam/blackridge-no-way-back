The first design of the GPU price (2026-11-08), kept as evidence of why it was replaced.

Each case was held 10 s in three alternating rounds. Apple's Metal Performance HUD logged every
frame's GPU time, but:
- the HUD logs only while the game's window is in front. It stopped after 32 s in the first run
  (first_run_hud_stopped.log), and left gaps of 7-13 s in the second, some while Claude Code ran
  commands. Some cases got no frames;
- with every effect off, GPU time drifted between rounds by more than the budgets: 20.5 to
  25.8 ms a frame from the driver's seat, 22.2 to 27.5 ms behind the van (RESULT.txt). The
  rounds' prices therefore ranged across zero, from -2.7 to +3.2 ms.

So the measurement was redesigned: each effect is shown and hidden in alternating short blocks,
kept running while hidden, and priced by the difference between neighbouring blocks
(../RESULT.txt). This first design's script and parser are kept here as gpu_price_v1.gd.txt
and gpu_price_v1.py.txt; the RESULT.txt here came from the parser as of the second run.
