Step 2c-1: why the yard changed Mission 1's fall (2026-11-11, Claude Code)

The yard made test_collision.gd 43/44: "Leaning shows the road from 1.9 m past the bumper, where a
harder hit leaves him (2.9 m from the seat)" needs a gain of at least 1.0 m and measures 0.98 m.
The struck man's fall at the bus stop's crossing, far from the shed, changed with the yard.

Method: probes/collision_precise.gd is test_collision.gd with the lean check printing three
decimals (its condition is the old strict "> 1.0", so it reports 43/44 even at a gain of exactly
1.000) and extra PRECISE lines: the gain measured at the height of his hips and at the road under
him. Every run: Godot 4.7.2 headless, --fixed-fps 60, HOME=.scratch-home. "Clone" runs are on an
APFS clone of game/ with one change, named below. Each log's "comes to rest", "lies ... ahead" and
first "PRECISE at x" lines give the fall.

The two falls seen
  old (2b r2, committed)  rests 3.7 s, lies 2.4 m out, hips 0.168 m above the road,
                          gain at his hips 1.000 m, at the road under him 1.10-1.12 m
  new (2c-1)              rests 5.3 s, lies 2.0 m out, hips 0.170 m above the road,

  (../collision_rerun/, ../collision_marks/lean_road.log, lean_road_2b_r2.log)

Reading (a claim from these runs, not a check): the fall depends on the bodies the world builds,
not on anything near him. The first extra body added while the world is built switches the old
fall to the new one, wherever it is, even 50 m underground; no further change tried (about 30)
moved the new fall. Bodies created after the build, or never placed in the world, change nothing.
The engine's reason was not found. Everything the player sees holds in both falls: from the seat
the hood hides him, leaning shows him (0 and 9 of 11 parts in the new fall).
