### What the current model gets wrong
1. Door 5.8 x 5.2 m = a drive-in door; a dock door is about 3.0 x 3.0 m starting at dock level (top about 4.25 m above the yard).
2. Flat doors; real ones show 4-6 horizontal sections with fine ribs, a row of windows, pale grey or silver.
3. 8 m centres; real 3.7-4.3 m, so the 36 m front holds 7-8 bays.
4. Concrete blocks 6.5 m wide x 2.2 m deep are wrong: docks are flush (the wall below the door is the dock face, leveller in a pit inside, bumpers outside, UK letter box) or in clad loading houses 3.3-3.6 m wide.
5. No shelters: each truck dock needs a black shelter about 3.4 x 3.5 m projecting 0.6-0.9 m.
6. Brick walls: sheds are metal panels over a concrete plinth (brick reads as old building or office; the agent's judgement).
7. Small high windows: daylight from rooflights; windows belong to the office.
8. Missing at night: lights over doors, signal lights, door numbers, yellow guards, drains, wear; roof needs a parapet line and downpipes.

### Suggestion for the front (the agent's)
- Building about 50 x 30 m, eaves about 8.5 m, 6 deg roof behind a 1 m parapet with a dark coping.
- 8 truck docks at 4.0 m centres, then the van bay at the end at 4.5 m, about 4 m of solid wall each end (personnel door, office). With the yard 36 m wide: 6 docks + the van bay.
- Dock flush, floor 1.25 m, precast concrete dock face, two black bumpers, leveller pit, yellow-and-black edge.
- Door insulated sectional 3.0 x 3.0 m, four 750 mm sections, RAL 9006 or 9002, four windows about 600 x 300 mm.
- Shelter black curtain 3,450 x 3,400 x 600 mm, 600 mm side curtains, 1,000 mm head curtain, white guide stripes, top 4.5 m above the yard.
- Van bay: same door at dock height, the planned dock lift in its pit, a van cushion shelter.
- Cladding: concrete plinth; dark grey micro-rib panels horizontally up to the shelter tops (about 5 m); light grey or silver trapezoidal above; downpipes every 2-3 bays.
- Lights: IP65 LED above each shelter aimed down, red/green at each door, door number above each shelter, floodlights about 8 m.

### Not confirmed
No European door-spacing standard (only UK vendor 3.7-4.0 m); yellow dock edge is guidance; plinth height from one project; high-speed doors at docks only vendor sources; EN 12464-2 table unread.

## Part 2. Free models and textures a sold game may use

Licences read on each asset's own page or the site's own record (Sketchfab via api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/<id>; Fab via listing data). Unverified (could not read): OpenGameArt (403), TurboSquid search (403), CGTrader listings (client-side, empty), BIMobject product pages and ARCAT terms (403). Sketchfab "Free Standard" skipped (not an allowed licence). Logos: only where noted; textures not inspected. Sketchfab "CC Attribution" = CC BY 4.0; downloads glTF/GLB + original. Poly Haven: Blend, glTF, FBX, USD. BlendKit: .blend. Every CC-BY asset needs a credit line.

### Store and maker licences
- Fab: licence summary (fab.com/eula, non-binding; full EULA unread): Standard allows commercial use, "usage is not limited to Unreal Engine"; Personal/Professional "same scope of rights"; no resale/redistribution of the asset alone. Many free listings are CC-BY instead. Listings moved from the UE Marketplace may carry the old licence (the listing says so).
- BlendKit (formerly BlenderKit): free "Royalty Free" allows commercial use without credit, not resale "in the same form"; games allowed "if these can't be extracted by the users in an easy way". Not CC0/CC-BY, so outside C11.
- TurboSquid: free = same Royalty Free License; in games models "must be contained in proprietary formats so that they cannot be opened or imported in a publicly available software application or framework" (II.7b) - a plain Godot .pck probably does not meet that. "Editorial Uses Only" cannot go in a video game.
- CGTrader: a free model carries the seller's chosen licence (s.24); Royalty Free requires "all commercially reasonable measures" against extraction, e.g. encryption (21A.3); Editorial is news only.
- Manufacturer BIM/CAD: BIMobject (Hormann, Overhead Door, Wayne Dalton, Loading Systems) personal use only "not for any commercial purposes" or construction documents; forbids putting content "into a product or service you provide to a third party" (4.4, 4.7g). ARCAT (Blue Giant, Poweramp): construction documents only (search snippet; terms page 403). GrabCAD (Larson swing-arm dock lights): commercial use needs creator's permission. Rite-Hite: BIM via Dodge Sweets or a contact form. Exception: Maxwell autodoors publishes its own models on Sketchfab under CC BY.

### Summary
1. Best overall: Poly Haven CC0 (Rollershutter Door, Security Light, Covered Car) + CC0 textures from Poly Haven and ambientCG.
2. Dock equipment: Maxwell autodoors' CC BY (seal, leveller, overhead door) and Fab's two free CC-BY kits (BEL1UM dock kit, Daonware sectional-door warehouse); check quality in Blender first.
3. Gaps: dock shelter, arm light, standalone bumper, truck chock, curtain-side trailer, sandwich-panel texture - model them. Manufacturer BIM terms forbid use in a sold game.
