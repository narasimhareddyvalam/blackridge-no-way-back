## Changes

Claude Code made these changes in Blender on 2026-10-31, at the author's request (decision C22):

- **Fitted to the van's cab:**
  - the dashboard is widened and extended to the windshield;
  - the doors, seats and A-pillars are moved;
  - the roof is stretched;
  - the cab is closed behind the seats.
- **Removed:** the Honda badges and the pictures on the screens.
- **Slimmed:** 935,427 faces became 847,364 triangles, compared against the original in nine
  views with no visible change.
- **Textures:** resized to 1024 and 2048 pixels.
- **Files:** split into four glTF files (front, back, roof, wheel), plus two simplified
  shadow stand-ins.
- **Moon roof (Run 16, decision C25):** the plate that had closed the roof's own sunroof
  frame is cut away inside that frame, in the roof file and the static shadow stand-in, so
  the frame opens onto glass and sky. The roof gained 44 triangles (847,408 in total).

The game shows its own gauges, map and mirror views on the screens.
