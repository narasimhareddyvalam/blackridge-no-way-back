# The gatehouse booths' models

The Sketchfab models, CC BY 4.0; the Poly Haven ones (CC0) are listed in `POLYHAVEN.md`.

- `dell_monitor`: "Monitor Dell P2314H" by GregorJan (Sketchfab `login166`), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/monitor-dell-p2314h-c69f09bb812e46cdbd5bf6c62499b848, licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: sized by its width to the real P2314H's 0.541 m (0.18 m deep, 0.43 m tall on its stand at its lowest), its screen facing glTF's front and named `Screen` (the game shows the guard's cameras on it), its base centred at the origin, its edge-only meshes dropped; 884 faces. Credited in the game's
  credits ("THE GATEHOUSES").
- `keyboard, mouse`: "PC Setup - Game Ready" by Melon Polygons (Sketchfab `Melonpolygons`), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pc-setup-game-ready-4126abfe9895488181dbde961e836788, licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: only its keyboard and mouse kept, cut from its one mesh by their loose parts and scaled together by the keyboard's width to 0.441 m (a compact full keyboard such as Logitech's MK270, 441 x 149 mm), each its own file (`keyboard`, 4,900 faces; `mouse`, 0.127 m long, 3,886 faces), its textures halved to 1024 pixels. Credited in the game's
  credits ("THE GATEHOUSES").
- `desk_phone`: "Corded Phone With Buttons" by dudecon (Sketchfab `dudecon`), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/corded-phone-with-buttons-b11bc340cda14a75b1d8c1d63e79f8dd, licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: sized to 0.22 m long, its keypad toward glTF's front, standing on the origin; 942 faces. Credited in the game's
  credits ("THE GATEHOUSES").
- `walkie_talkie`: "Walkie Talkie" by barism09 (Sketchfab `barism09`), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/walkie-talkie-5d57f97915a844f584157cb86a474eba, licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: sized to 0.17 m tall with its aerial (a handheld PMR radio), its face toward glTF's front, standing on the origin; 6,800 faces. Credited in the game's
  credits ("THE GATEHOUSES").
- `ac_indoor_unit`: "Indoor air conditioner unit" by Rylae Shylna (Sketchfab `risteralline`), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/indoor-air-conditioner-unit-d93c5557a9ba46afbb00e35f48343077, licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changed: sized to 0.80 m wide (a split system's wall head; 0.20 m deep, 0.27 m tall), its louvre toward glTF's front, its textures cut from 4096 to 1024 pixels; 528 faces. Credited in the game's
  credits ("THE GATEHOUSES").
- `draughtsman_chair`: "Office Chair" by Red Fox (Sketchfab `nokillnando`),
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/office-chair-b228a29fa84544c2be501c295653ffe7, licensed CC BY
  4.0, the depot's copy (`art/depot/office_chair.glb`) changed for a 0.88 m gatehouse counter:
  everything above its gas lift's middle (0.25 m) raised 0.17 m, so its cushion is 0.64 m up as a
  draughtsman's chair's is, its lift stretched, and a chrome foot ring 0.46 m across on a collar and
  three spokes added 0.24 m up. Credited ("THE GATEHOUSES").
