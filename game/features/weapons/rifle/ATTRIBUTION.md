# Rifle

`m16.glb`: "M16 Assault Rifle" by jeandiz on Sketchfab
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/339d0f7b21024387853dd926a5d51b50), licensed CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: scaled to its real length (1.00 m), its
4K textures halved to 2K and written as WebP, exported as glTF from Blender 5.2.2 on 2026-11-22
for Assignment 7 (#393-#394). Its parts (body, magazine, bolt carrier, dust cover, charging handle,
sights, rail, barrel, pistol grip) are kept as separate meshes so the reload can move them.
