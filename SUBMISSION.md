Known limitations: Godot's GPU timer reads 0 on Metal, so GPU time came from Apple's Metal Performance HUD. B2's exhaust is over its GPU budget (1.11 ms against 0.30). The frame itself takes about 26 ms of GPU time from the driver's seat at 1280×800 on the MacBook Air's Retina display (about 39 frames a second), above a 60 FPS frame before any effect; the world changes and the display's pixels are the likely causes, unmeasured. The Iveco model has no tailpipe, so B2's exhaust position is assumed (brief revision 2). B1's real-size splash droplets are faint from a few metres, and its slowest drops fade 2–4 m above the ground (TUNING.md). A headless run draws no particles and runs no GPU particle process, so headless prices are near zero.
